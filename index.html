Featured capstone project

Proposed application · HouseFactor

Compare homes with more confidence.

HouseFactor is a planned real estate comparison and decision-support application. Building on my previous mobile event-tracking coursework, the goal is to help users organize properties, manage showings, save observations, and compare homes using their own priorities.

Rather than relying on a single overall rating, users will be able to adjust the importance of factors such as price, condition, and location. The application will then produce a clear, explainable ranking.
